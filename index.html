01 — About the club

Built by students who wanted more than a certificate.

AI Nexus BVM started because a handful of students at BVM were tired of watching AI happen elsewhere and decided to build it on campus instead — real models, real projects, real deadlines, none of it graded.

We run on one rule: learn in public, build in the open. Every workshop ships something. Every project has a repo. Every member leaves with more than notes.

What we build in

Six working groups, each run by members who actually ship in that space. Pick one, or drift between all of them.

TRACK / 01

Machine Learning

Core ML foundations — regression to gradient boosting — taught through Kaggle-style problems, not slideshows.

TRACK / 02

Computer Vision

Detection, segmentation and image pipelines — building things that actually see and respond.

TRACK / 03

NLP & LLMs

From tokenizers to fine-tuning open models — understanding language models by breaking them apart.

TRACK / 04

Generative AI

Diffusion models, image synthesis and applied GenAI tooling — where research meets weekend builds.

TRACK / 05

Robotics & IoT

Where AI meets hardware — sensor fusion, embedded inference and machines that move on their own.

TRACK / 06

Data Science

Cleaning, wrangling and visualising real datasets — the unglamorous work that makes models possible.

How the club runs

No fixed syllabus — just a rhythm of hands-on sessions built around what members want to ship next.

Weekly

Build Sessions

Open lab hours where members work on live projects together — bring a laptop and a problem, leave with progress and a pull request.

Bi-weekly

Workshops & Deep Dives

Hands-on sessions on a single topic — fine-tuning a model, building a CV pipeline, prompt engineering that actually works.

Seasonal

Hackathons & Project Sprints

Team up, pick a problem statement, ship a working prototype in a weekend. Best builds get showcased across campus.

Ongoing

Guest Talks & Industry Sessions

Engineers and researchers walk through what they actually work on — and what they wish they knew as students.

Membership is open
